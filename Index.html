Expandable checklist for practical delivery and technical assurance.

Summary

“The fundamental change is moving from a card-centric model to an account-centric model. In the existing CBT model, the card and field equipment carry more of the value and product logic. In ABT, the credential mainly identifies the account, while account, journey and fare processing are managed centrally.

For migration, I would first assess the existing cards, products, balances, entitlements, devices and interfaces. Then define the target architecture, data mapping and coexistence strategy. After that, I would validate the migration through testing, controlled pilots and phased rollout, while monitoring transaction integrity, fare calculation and revenue reconciliation.

The key objective is to complete the transformation without disrupting passenger service and without losing customer, transaction or revenue integrity.”

Final mental model

Customer → Credential → Validator → Event → Account → Journey → Fare → Payment → Reconciliation.
